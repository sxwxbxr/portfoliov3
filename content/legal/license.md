## Open-source packages

`@permitojs/core` and `@permitojs/react` are MIT-licensed and complete without Pro.

## Pro packages

- Licensed per person on your team. Your clients and their websites need no licence of their own.
- Freelancer covers one person. Agency and Lifetime cover up to ten people. For eleven or more, get in touch.
- No licence key and no phone-home. The packages never contact a server to check your licence.
- After cancelling, every version you already received keeps working. Only updates and repository access end.
- Checkout, invoices and VAT are handled by Polar as merchant of record.
