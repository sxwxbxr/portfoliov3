Evaluation methods used

Each success criterion was evaluated by a person with automated tests (axe-core, Surjection) on 1 page(s) and a manual checklist. Applicable standards: WCAG 2.2 level AA and EN 301 549 (clause 9, mapping version 2026.10).

Terms

  • Supports: the functionality of the product has at least one method that meets the criterion without known defects or meets it with equivalent facilitation.
  • Partially Supports: some functionality of the product does not meet the criterion.
  • Does Not Support: the majority of product functionality does not meet the criterion.
  • Not Applicable: the criterion is not relevant to the product.
  • Not Evaluated: the product has not been evaluated against the criterion.
