Serious Elements must meet minimum color contrast ratio thresholds

WCAG 1.4.3 (AA) · EN 301 549 9.1.4.3 · color-contrast · 1 element

Ensure the contrast between foreground and background colors meets WCAG 2 AA minimum contrast ratio thresholds

How to fix
  • p
    <p class="hours">Montag bis Samstag, 6 bis 18 Uhr</p>

    Fix any of the following:
    Element has insufficient color contrast of 2.01 (foreground color: #b5b5b5, background color: #fffdf8, font size: 12.0pt (16px), font weight: normal). Expected contrast ratio of 4.5:1
