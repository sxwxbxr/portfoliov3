Accessibility checklist: Bäckerei Muster

Check every criterion by hand. Issues found automatically are already marked as "fail". Your input is kept in this browser until you download the file.

1.1.1 Non-text Content A

How to check: Every informative image, icon and control has a text alternative that serves the same purpose; decorative images are hidden.

1.1.1

1.3.1 Info and Relationships A

How to check: Headings, lists, tables and form groups are marked up as such, not only styled to look like them.

1.3.1

1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) AA

How to check: Text has a contrast of at least 4.5:1, large text 3:1, including text on images and in hover and focus states.

1.4.11 Non-text Contrast AA

How to check: Borders of fields, focus indicators, icons and chart elements have a contrast of at least 3:1.

2.1.1 Keyboard A

How to check: Every function works with the keyboard alone: menus, dialogs, sliders, date pickers, drag and drop.

2.1.1

2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context) A

How to check: Link text, alone or with its sentence, says where the link goes; no bare "more" or "click here".

2.4.4

2.4.7 Focus Visible AA

How to check: The keyboard focus is always clearly visible.

2.4.7

3.1.1 Language of Page A

How to check: The page language is set correctly in the html element.

3.1.1

3.3.2 Labels or Instructions A

How to check: Every field has a visible label; required fields and formats are explained.

4.1.2 Name, Role, Value A

How to check: Custom controls expose name, role and state to assistive technology (check with a screen reader).
