Method

Each success criterion was evaluated by a person with the help of automated tests (axe-core, Surjection) and a manual checklist. The outcomes follow the terms of WCAG-EM: passed, failed, cannot tell, inapplicable. A criterion counts as passed only when a person decided so.

Limits

This report is not legal advice and does not confirm conformity with WCAG, EN 301 549 or the European Accessibility Act. EN 301 549 clause 9 corresponds to WCAG 2.1; criteria that exist only in WCAG 2.2 have no clause there. The mapping is maintained by Surjection and may change between versions.
