Agencies in Switzerland, Germany and Austria rarely need just one of these. A client website has to ask for cookie consent and meet accessibility rules, and a SaaS app has to show who changed what. From today the three Pro add-ons for that come as one licence: the [Compliance Bundle](https://packages.sweber.dev/bundles/compliance).

## What is included

- **Permito Pro**: themes, the service catalog, the cookie table, the consent log and the scanner for Permito, the self-hosted cookie banner.
- **Surjection Pro**: client reports in your agency's branding, history across projects and the guided manual checklist for Surjection, the accessibility toolkit.
- **Logarithm Pro**: tamper evidence, retention and erasure, export and forwarding for Logarithm, the self-hosted audit log.

Checkout gives your GitHub account access to the Pro repositories of all three. Installation works exactly as with a single Pro licence.

## What comes next

Two more packages are being built for the bundle: a withdrawal and cancellation button kit for the German and Austrian button laws (§ 356a and § 312k BGB), and labels for AI-generated content under Art. 50 of the EU AI Act. They join the bundle when they are released, with no higher price for running subscriptions.

## New prices for Cosine Pro and Derivative Pro

Cosine Pro and Derivative Pro now start at CHF 12 a month (Agency CHF 39, Lifetime CHF 1'290). At CHF 9, payment fees took about one franc in nine. Subscriptions that are already running keep the price they started with, and the yearly plans still cost the same as ten months.
